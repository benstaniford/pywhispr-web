1. Install the certificate

Download the certificate

On an iPhone, open this page in Safari — no other browser can install a profile. Tap the button, then Allow.

2. Approve the profile

Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → PyWhispr Web CA → Install. Enter your passcode and confirm the unsigned-profile warning.

3. Turn on full trust

This step is not optional. Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → enable PyWhispr Web CA. Until you do, iOS installs the certificate but still refuses to trust it, Safari keeps warning, and the microphone stays blocked — exactly the problem you are trying to fix.

4. Use the HTTPS address

Go to {{ https_url }} and check there is no warning, then add it to your home screen from the share menu. Bookmarking the plain-HTTP address will silently leave you without a microphone.
